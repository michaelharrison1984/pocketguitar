PLAY IT, THEN CHANGE IT

Three passes

  1. Listen twice. Hum the phrase before touching the guitar.
  2. Copy the rhythm and notes during the “Your turn” bar.
  3. In Adapt mode, land on the new chord’s third. Then invent your own opening.

Only the final note changes in Adapt mode. The earlier notes can create tension against the new chord; use the ending to resolve it.

Self-assessment only. The app does not listen to your guitar.

Count 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &. In shuffle phrases, the & falls later (long–short). A horizontal line holds the previous note. Notes in the same column sound together. The framed final note is your target.

Phrase in a scale box

Scroll sideways on small screens to see all frets. Numbers under each note show its order in the phrase. Square = final target. Dashed marker with b = bend pitch destination, not a new finger position. An arrow marks a slide or legato destination. Tap a note to check its pitch.

MAKE IT YOUR PRACTICE ROOM

Settings

App colours and fretboard colours are independent. Your custom note colours stay as you set them.

Fretboard colours & readability

Applies to every fretboard. Labels automatically switch between black and white. Square markers identify targets; colour is not the only clue.

Saved automatically in this browser, separately from your musical setups.

Screen & mobile practice

Uses your browser’s screen wake lock while this app is visible. On a phone, open the app over HTTPS. If unavailable, increase your phone’s screen timeout for the session. Manually locking the phone or switching apps still stops audio.

Your data — on this device

Saved setups, practice notes, themes and learning progress are stored locally in this device’s browser. They are never uploaded to the Docker server or written to its volume. Each device/browser has its own data. Keep using the same address and browser; clearing site data removes these saves. There is no cross-device sync.
